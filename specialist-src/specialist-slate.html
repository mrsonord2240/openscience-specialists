The scores, checked

The bar is THRESHOLD.md. It has nine gates: every bundled skill has a real audit of 75 or more, core skills score 85 or more, and nothing a skill points to is missing. Most of the work was finding out which audit numbers could be believed.

  • 131

    Scientific audit reports are templated. The inputs are named "Test case N", 104 reports share identical input totals, and they claim 86–92. The polish changelog says 75–79, so that number is used. The other 148 reports, including all 141 medical ones, are genuine.

  • 183

    *_audit_result_v2.json files are backfill. Their test inputs are sentences from the skill's own description, their notes cite a "legacy review", and every one scores 85 or more. They are not counted.

  • 157

    Referenced files were never shipped, across 81 skills, 80 of them scripts. They are missing upstream too: the Downloads archive is exactly f5ef65b9, and the F: copy matches it byte for byte. Full list: MISSING-FILES.md.

  • 10

    Skill IDs are shared with the published auto-research specialist. The App refuses a second skill with the same ID and different bytes, so those ten ship the published bytes, digest-verified.

Script defects the authoring agents found (✓ confirmed independently)
  • ✓ model-calibration-curve reports 1 − Harrell's C: concordance() without reverse = TRUE. Its sibling nomogram-construction gets this right.
  • ✓ meta-analysis Egger test returns the slope's p-value and SE, not the intercept's.
  • ✓ protocol-deviation-classifier's main.py does not parse (line 793).
  • mendelian-randomisation: the documented input keys crash it; F defaults to 0; MR-Egger shifts under allele recoding; "Steiger" uses no sample sizes.
  • adaptive-trial-simulator: Pocock early boundaries give a false-positive rate of about 0.048 against a 0.025 target.
  • clinical-trial-protocol-skill: 2:1 allocation sized at 239 instead of about 318.
  • sample-size-basic undersizes small groups (7 where the exact t-test gives 9). xgboost-analysis early-stops on its own test split.
  • decision-curve-analysis defaults to a case-control design with prevalence 0.3. randomization-gen has no seed option.
  • retraction-watcher counts failed lookups as "clear". The rebuttal and tone Skills insert "We have revised…" whatever was done.
  • adverse-event-narrative writes today's date as the report date. nsfc-grant-writer's guide says to "generate plausible preliminary work".
